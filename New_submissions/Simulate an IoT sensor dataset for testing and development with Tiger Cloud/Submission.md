Our team completed the IoT sensor dataset simulation challenge for MLH Global Hack Week: Data. We created a Tiger Cloud TimescaleDB service and built two tables: sensors for device metadata and sensor_data as a time-series hypertable. We simulated 24 hours of readings from 4 sensors at 5-minute intervals, generating around 1,156 temperature and CPU records. We then ran analytical queries to calculate averages, last values, and time-bucketed metrics for testing and development.
